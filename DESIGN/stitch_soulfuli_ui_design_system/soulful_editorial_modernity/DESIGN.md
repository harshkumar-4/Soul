## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an intersection of literary editorial publication, architectural restraint, and introspective warmth. It rejects the generic pastel aesthetics of modern mental-health SaaS, avoiding sterile clinic motifs, clinical dashboards, and esoteric iconography. Instead, the interface operates as a quiet physical journal or an enduring broadsheet—grounded, intelligent, dignified, and serene.

### Tone & Sensibility
- **Dignified Contemplation:** Visual pacing prioritizes room for thought. Whitespace is an intentional, active design element that creates calm rather than void.
- **Organic Materiality:** Surfaces mimic tactile, premium, untreated paper stock and stone, eschewing synthetic blurs, glossy surfaces, and loud gradient sweeps.
- **Quiet Authority:** Interactions are restrained, precise, and deliberate. Micro-interactions do not bounce or clamor for attention; they dissolve, shift slightly, and settle.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic editorial atmosphere rooted in earth minerals, unbleached fibers, and deep vegetal dyes.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary / Anchors (`#1F2A24`):** Deep Forest provides structural grounding, high-contrast readability, and an ink-like weight for primary display headlines and commanding actions.
- **Secondary / Foliage Accent (`#7D8B78`):** Muted Sage is used for balanced secondary structures, contemplative states, soft badges, and harmonious natural accents.
- **Tertiary / Intention Accent (`#C86D51`):** Terracotta acts as an emotional punctuation point, applied sparingly to pull-quote indicators, active states, key milestone highlights, and warm calls to reflection.
- **Neutral / Canvas Ground (`#FAF6EE`):** Warm Ivory forms the foundational reading surface, reducing optical glare compared to pure white and providing a tactile, literary paper finish.

### Functional Roles
- **Surface Sand (`#EFE9DE`):** Tier-two container fills, input field beds, and subtle module differentiation.
- **Text Primary (`#1C1E1D`):** Deep Charcoal ink for body text and long-form prose.
- **Text Secondary (`#5C605C`):** Muted Stone for metadata, captions, section indexes, and contemplative secondary copy.
- **Subtle Earth Rule (`#DDD6C8`):** Hairline dividing lines and structural borders.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional dialogue between classic editorial prose and clean, humanist interface utility.

### Headline & Display Philosophy
Headlines employ **Newsreader** to invoke the craft of literary journals and contemplative broadsheets. Titles balance regular upright forms with selective italicization of core concepts (e.g., *intention*, *presence*, *meaning*) to impart tone and cadence.

### Body & UI Application
Interface layers, body paragraphs, and microcopy use **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering high legibility and geometric balance that counterweights the expressive serif. Body copy uses relaxed line heights (minimum 1.6x) to preserve open vertical rhythm across dense reading passages.

### Metadata & Indexing
Section headers and tags use small, tracked uppercase typography (`label-md` and `label-sm`), styled with uppercase transforms and generous tracking (`0.12em` to `0.14em`) in Muted Stone to delineate page sections like book folios.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an asymmetric, editorial 12-column grid that avoids the mechanical symmetry of application dashboards, drawing inspiration from printed essay layouts.

### Structural Flow
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column grid, 64px outer margins, 32px gutters. Content width caps at 1240px for interfaces and 740px for single-column long-form reading modules.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid, 32px outer margins, 24px gutters.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column grid, 24px outer margins, 16px gutters.

### Rhythm & Composition
- Layouts alternate between compact editorial modules and wide, unhurried negative space.
- Sections are delimited by delicate 1px hairline rules (`#DDD6C8`) rather than heavy backgrounds or full-width box fills.
- Text blocks and adjacent images use asymmetrical column ratios (e.g., 7:5 or 8:4) to emulate contemporary art and culture magazines.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids high-contrast drop shadows, floating card blurs, and skeuomorphic drop-relief. Depth is physical, flat, and tactile, achieved through subtle shifts in paper tone and hairline perimeter definitions.

### Visual Depth Techniques
- **Paper Layering:** Surfaces exist on distinct tones rather than spatial heights. Base canvas (`#FAF6EE`) supports secondary content fields in Soft Sand (`#EFE9DE`), creating organic stratification without synthetic shadows.
- **Hairline Boundaries:** Containment is marked by 1px rules tinted with `#DDD6C8`, producing the quiet precision of bookbinding.
- **Focused Ambient Shadow:** Modals and elevated flyouts use a muted, warm ambient shadow: `0 12px 36px -8px rgba(31, 42, 36, 0.08)`. The tint draws from Deep Forest, preventing cold gray shadows from polluting the warm ivory field.

## Shapes

The shape system is tailored to soft, architectural geometry with understated 4px corner curves (`roundedness: 1`).

### Geometry Rules
- **Base Components:** Form inputs, cards, action items, and notification modules use soft, structured 4px corners (`0.25rem`), retaining an architectural crispness reminiscent of archival stationery.
- **Large Containers:** Modals and highlighted editorial hero banners scale to 8px (`0.5rem`).
- **Capsule Pills:** Reserved exclusively for status indicators, category tags, and thematic chips to create contrast against structured square cards.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Button:** Deep Forest background (`#1F2A24`), Warm Ivory text (`#FAF6EE`), 4px radius. Padding: 14px 28px. Typographic style: `body-sm` bold. Hover introduces a calm transition to `#2A3A31` with no displacement or scale bouncing.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface Sand (`#EFE9DE`) background with Deep Charcoal text (`#1C1E1D`), encased in a 1px `#DDD6C8` border.
- **Ghost / Editorial Link Button:** Deep Charcoal text with an underlined 1px hairline rule separated by 4px baseline offset; on hover, the rule shifts color to Terracotta (`#C86D51`).

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Container:** Background of `#EFE9DE`, flat with no shadow. Border: 1px hairline `#DDD6C8`.
- **States:** Active/Focus shifts the border to Deep Forest (`#1F2A24`) with an interior 1px stroke. No saturated blue or synthetic glow outlines.
- **Typography:** Labels use `label-md` tracked small-caps positioned above the field in Muted Stone.

### Cards & Editorial Modules
- **Story & Insight Cards:** Grounded on Warm Ivory or Soft Sand. 1px `#DDD6C8` framing, 28px internal padding. Image assets maintain natural 4:5 or 3:2 aspect ratios with desaturated, natural tones.
- **Accordion Modules:** Flat stacked rows divided by horizontal hairline borders. Icon indicator is a delicate, thin 12px serif glyph (`+` / `—`). Expanding panels animate downward with linear ease.

### Reflection & Quotation Blocks
- **Pull Quotes:** Newsreader italic type (`quote-lg`) rendered in Deep Forest. Left-bordered with a 2px vertical rule in Terracotta (`#C86D51`), set 24px off the text margin.
- **Reflection Prompts:** Self-contained contemplative boxes using a soft sand backdrop (`#EFE9DE`), subtle inset spacing (32px), and a centered, tracked category label.

### Navigation & Header
- **Top Bar:** Fixed minimalist masthead on Warm Ivory with 95% opacity and minimal backdrop blur to softly separate it from scrolling content. Bottom 1px hairline border in `#DDD6C8`.
- **Wordmark & Links:** Wordmark set in Newsreader Regular. Links styled in `body-sm` Deep Charcoal with generous 32px horizontal spacing.

### Footer
- **Structure:** Grounded in deep forest (`#1F2A24`) with warm ivory typography (`#FAF6EE`), functioning as a meditative closing note for the page. Features an unhurried reflection prompt, an unobtrusive single-input dispatch signup, and a balanced multi-column sitemap index.
